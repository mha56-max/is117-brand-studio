# Clean Cut — Research Review

**Research date:** November 12, 2026  
**Project:** Clean Cut — Plain White T-Shirt

## 1. Research Question

How do clothing brands present a basic T-shirt so customers can understand its fit, fabric, comfort, and everyday usefulness before choosing to buy it?

## 2. Competitor Research

### UNIQLO — Crew Neck T-Shirt

**Source:** https://www.uniqlo.com/us/en/products/E424873-000/00  
**Accessed:** November 12, 2026

**Observation:** UNIQLO describes the T-shirt's cotton material, smooth finish, and balanced fit. The page provides product details and sizing information.

**What I learned:** Customers benefit when material and fit information is easy to find.

**Clean Cut design decision:** Explain the product clearly and make sizing and product details easy to understand.

### Everlane — The Box-Cut Tee

**Source:** https://www.everlane.com/products/womens-organic-cotton-box-cut-tee-everglade  
**Accessed:** November 12, 2026

**Observation:** Everlane describes its fabric, relaxed fit, cropped length, and sizing. Its product page also uses conversational language and styling information.

**What I learned:** A friendly brand voice and specific fit information can help customers understand what they are considering.

**Clean Cut design decision:** Use simple, friendly wording and explain the product without exaggerating its qualities.

### Gap — Modern Crewneck T-Shirt

**Source:** https://www.gap.com/browse/product.do?pid=540635172  
**Accessed:** November 12, 2026

**Observation:** Gap highlights the shirt's cotton-modal blend, stretch, fit options, and size guide. The page also displays customer review information.

**What I learned:** Fit options and visible customer feedback can help shoppers evaluate a product.

**Clean Cut design decision:** Make sizing information easy to locate. Do not invent customer reviews or ratings.

## 3. Audience Research

These are audience hypotheses, not validated customer research.

**Primary audience — Everyday Student**

A college student looking for an affordable white T-shirt that works for classes and everyday outfits. This customer values simplicity, comfort, and clear product information.

**Secondary audience — Gift Buyer**

Someone purchasing a white T-shirt for another person who needs clear sizing information and reassurance before choosing.

**Chosen audience:** Everyday Student. Clean Cut focuses on making an everyday basic simple to understand and choose.

## 4. Brand and Design Decisions

Clean Cut's promise is: **A plain white tee, made simple to choose.**

The brand uses the Sage archetype and a calm, clear, honest voice.

The website should feel minimal, comfortable, and easy to navigate. Product details, sizing, and the demo bag should be straightforward.

The research supports making product information easy to find rather than relying on exaggerated claims.

## 5. Honest Persuasion

- **Commitment and consistency:** Choosing a size before adding the shirt to the demo bag creates a simple, voluntary shopping sequence.
- **Liking:** Calm, friendly language makes the experience approachable without using fake reviews or pressure.

## 6. Evidence and Limitations

The competitor observations above come from the linked retailer product pages. These are examples of how the brands present their products, not independent proof of product quality.

The audience descriptions are hypotheses. I have not yet conducted customer interviews or usability testing to validate them.

Prices, product availability, reviews, and product descriptions may change.
